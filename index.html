Management software built for climbing gyms

Run your gym with confidence

Traverse is an all-in-one platform purpose-built for climbing gyms and bouldering halls. From memberships and check-in to your point of sale and events — everything in one place, designed for the way your gym actually works.

What Traverse does

🧗

Memberships & Entries

Manage all your membership types, track entries, and handle renewals with ease. Whether climbers pay per visit or hold a monthly pass, Traverse keeps it organised and automated.

🛒

Point of Sale

A fast, intuitive POS for your front desk. Sell day passes, memberships, gear, and drinks in a single transaction. Built for the pace of a busy gym entrance.

📅

Events

Plan and run competitions, clinics, and workshops. Members can register through the app, and you get full oversight of attendance and capacity.

📱

Guest Mobile App

A branded iOS and Android app for your climbers. They can manage their membership, register for events, and check their visit history — all from their phone.

About Traverse

Traverse Software was built from the ground up for climbing gyms and bouldering halls. We know that a busy gym needs software that stays out of the way and just works — at the front desk, on the floor, and on your members' phones.

Our platform combines a powerful backoffice web application for your staff with a smooth mobile experience for your guests, all backed by a solid API that keeps everything in sync.
